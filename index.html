About Liberty Tailors & Drapers

Liberty Tailors & Drapers has been serving customers since 1997. We specialize in premium coat, blazer, shirt, pant and safari suit stitching with excellent fitting and craftsmanship.

We also provide Home Service, Fabric with Stitching, Alteration & Repair and Urgent Stitching.
